## Definition
Diagnosing a dead/broken VRM is a systematic check of input power, MOSFETs, drivers, controller, output capacitors, and the CPU itself. Uses a multimeter, oscilloscope, thermal camera, and visual inspection.

## Center: Diagnostic Flowchart
SYMPTOM → visual check → input voltage → short-circuit test → MOSFET test → controller → caps → conclusion (which part to replace).

## Step-by-Step Diagnosis
1. **Visual inspection** — burnt MOSFET? discolored PCB? cracked/bulged capacitor?
2. **Multimeter (PSU OFF)** — diode mode on every MOSFET (HS + LS + gate)
3. **Check input** — 12 V present on the EPS12V 4-pin / 8-pin connector
4. **Check standby** — 5 VSB present at ATX pin 9
5. **Look for shorts** — Vcore rail to GND reading **< 0.5 V on diode range = short**
6. **Isolate the shorted component** — thermal camera (heat = shorted part) or freeze spray (cool = shorted part)
7. **Test the CPU** — remove and try in another known-good board
8. **PWM controller VCC** — should be ~5 V or 12 V depending on chip
9. **Gate signals** — check with oscilloscope, should be clean PWM square wave
10. **Replace the bad part** — SPS / DrMOS / MOSFET / controller / PWM IC

## Common Failures
- Shorted HS-MOSFET (gate shorted to drain)
- Dead PWM controller (no switching output at all)
- Cracked SMD output cap (visible bulge or leak)
- Burnt inductor (rare but seen on abused boards)
- Open gate resistor (no gate drive to FET)
- Cold solder joint at high-current pin (intermittent)
- Bent CPU socket pin (short between Vcore and GND)
- ESD damage to controller or driver

## Tools Needed
- Digital multimeter (Fluke 87V recommended)
- Thermal camera (FLIR One Pro or similar)
- Oscilloscope (100 MHz+ bandwidth)
- DC electronic load (for stress testing)
- Hot-air rework station
- Freeze spray (component isolation)
- Magnifying lamp / microscope
- ESR meter (capacitor test)

## Troubleshooting Cases

| Case | Symptom | Likely Cause | Action |
|------|---------|--------------|--------|
| 1 | No Vcore, no POST | EPS12V not connected, 5VSB missing, short on Vcore | Check PSU cables, multimeter Vcore-GND |
| 2 | Boot loop / restart | VR_RDY not asserted, Vcore ripple too high | Scope Vcore, check bulk caps |
| 3 | One phase dead | Identified by thermal camera | Bad SPS or missing gate signal |
| 4 | Burnt smell | Smoke residue location | Replace burnt FET and neighbors |

## Key Insight
> **90 % of "dead board" issues are SHORT CIRCUITS.**
> Always check the Vcore rail resistance to GND first — it should be **> 50 Ω** on diode range.
> A reading of **< 5 Ω** means a short, and a thermal camera will show which component is heating up.
